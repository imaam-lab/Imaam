About

More than design. A mindset.

Introduction

I’m Mohamed Imaam — designer, developer and founder from Sri Lanka.

I create brand identities, graphics and websites for people and businesses that want their work to be clear, considered and credible. With 3+ years of experience and 15+ completed projects, I combine visual thinking with practical digital skills.

Professional journey

My practice spans graphic design, logo and brand identity, web design, web development and UI/UX. Alongside client and concept work, I founded two creative businesses — Weelen Studio and Desycode Graphics — and continue to learn through structured training, including certificates from Canva Design School earned in October 2026.

Design philosophy

Strategy before decoration. I start with the audience, message and objective, then use only what the work needs. Details are chosen with intention, so the result feels simple, consistent and purposeful.

Working principles

How I work.

01

Approach to technology

Tools serve ideas.

I work with tools such as Figma, Canva and Visual Studio Code, and use AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude to move faster. Technology accelerates the process; judgement, taste and responsibility stay with me.

Long-term vision

To keep growing Weelen Studio and Desycode Graphics into businesses that deliver impact through design and technology.
